# Campus Carpool Matching and Reservation System

## Project Description

A Java-based website designed to make commuting more convenient and affordable for university students. It provides a platform where students can connect with fellow students traveling along similar routes and destinations, allowing them to share available vehicle seats and transportation expenses. The system features ride posting, carpool matching, seat reservations, and a cost-sharing calculator to simplify transportation arrangements. By promoting ride-sharing, the project aims to reduce students' commuting expenses, maximize vehicle occupancy, and help minimize traffic congestion around the university.

## Problem Being Addressed

Many college students commute daily to university, with some coming from nearby communities, whilst others come from distant municipalities. Students who live far from their universities often face longer travel times, higher transportation expenses, and limited commuting options. Meanwhile, many students who have access to private vehicles travel alone, leaving several passenger seats unoccupied despite other students potentially traveling along the same routes. Specifically, these situations contribute to the following problems such as:

1. **High Transportation Expenses:** Students who use public transportation often have to pay higher fares, ranging from 30 to 50 pesos or higher, depending on where they are coming from. On the other hand, students who drive private cars shoulder the high costs of fuel, parking fees, and other transportation expenses on their own. Both situations create a significant financial burden for students who have to travel to campus and attend classes regularly.

2. **Increased Traffic Congestion:** Having multiple students travel separately using private vehicles increases the number of vehicles on the road, contributing to traffic congestion, particularly during peak school hours and along major roads leading to the university.

3. **Underutilized Vehicle Capacity:** Many private vehicles travel with vacant passenger seats that could otherwise accommodate students heading toward the same destination or passing through similar routes.

## Project Objectives

Campus Carpool Matching and Reservation System will provide a convenient and affordable transportation solution by connecting students. Specifically, this project aims to:

1. Develop a platform where students can find and connect with carpool partners.
2. Allow student drivers to post available rides and passengers to reserve seats based on their preferred routes and schedules.
3. Match students traveling along similar routes, including those coming from distant municipalities.
4. Provide a cost-sharing calculator to help distribute transportation expenses among passengers.
5. Encourage ride-sharing to reduce commuting costs, maximize vehicle capacity, and help minimize traffic congestion.

## Target Users

Campus Carpool Matching and Reservation System is intended for college students who regularly commute, particularly those who travel from both nearby and distant places. Specifically, this system is intended to:

1. **Student Drivers** – Students who own or have access to private vehicles and are willing to offer available seats to fellow students.
2. **Student Passengers** – Students who are looking for affordable and convenient transportation by sharing rides with others traveling along similar routes.

## Main Features

The main features of the Campus Carpool Matching and Reservation System are:

1. **Student Registration and Login** – Allows students to create accounts and securely access the website.
2. **Student Profile Management** – Enables users to manage their personal information and transportation preferences.
3. **Carpool Matching** – Matches students based on similar routes, destinations, and travel schedules.
4. **Ride Posting** – Allows student drivers to post available rides with details such as departure location, destination, date, time, and available seats.
5. **Ride Reservation** – Enables student passengers to search for available rides and reserve seats.
6. **Available Seat Management** – Automatically updates the number of available seats after reservations or cancellations.
7. **Cost-Sharing Calculator** – Estimates and divides transportation expenses among passengers.
8. **Ride History** – Allows users to view their previous and upcoming carpool reservations.
9. **Admin Dashboard** – Provides administrators with tools to manage users, monitor ride postings, and oversee system activities.
